Behavioral Programming expert catalog

We are collecting Behavioral Programming debugging scenarios and potential bad smells for expert review.

Debugging scenarios describe behavioral failures observed through executions. Bad smells are separate, suspicious patterns found through model structure, execution evidence, or both; they are not necessarily bugs.

The task

Help us refine both catalogs

What to send

Any distinct BP bug type or potential bad smell in free form. Code, traces, screenshots, diagrams, and short notes are welcome.

Timing

We plan to keep this collection phase open for two weeks. A reminder will be sent after about a week and a half.

Goal

We will use bug types to construct a debugging dataset. Bad smells form a separate catalog of candidates for BP linting, runtime checks, and model review.

Methodology

Our methodology combines execution-based analysis of failures with structural review of requests, waits, blocks, and event relationships.

Acknowledgments

Thank you for helping shape the catalogs

We thank the BP community, researchers, lecturers, and practitioners who review these examples and contribute additional bug types or potential bad smells. Your feedback will help make the debugging dataset and bad-smell catalog more useful.
